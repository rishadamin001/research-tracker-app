# Privacy

Project questions, notes, evidence extraction, and protocols are stored in browser IndexedDB. No app account, central project server, or analytics is used. There is no application-level encryption. Anyone with access to the browser profile may access the data. Clearing browser storage can erase it.

Search queries are sent to OpenAlex; DOI lookups to Crossref. With an optional gateway, its operator and provider infrastructure receive the requests. Google Fonts receives font requests and normal network metadata. External-link destinations have their own policies. Do not enter sensitive participant information into search queries.

Back up regularly and use one editing tab. Imported backups are validated and added as new copies. Deleting a project removes its local workspace record, not downloaded backups or copies on other devices. No guarantee of secure forensic erasure is made.
