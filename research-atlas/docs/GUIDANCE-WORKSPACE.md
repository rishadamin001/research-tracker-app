# Guidance, focus and research pathways

The visible workspace choices are **Step-by-step guidance** and **Focus workspace**. Their stored values remain `guided` and `expert`, so older preferences and backup settings are compatible. Mode is presentation, not a different project or an assessment of researcher expertise.

Question guidance shows problem, scope, wording and objectives in four small groups, with examples and recorded-ingredient checks. Focus shows the same grouped fields directly. Methods similarly separates feasibility, design/procedures and ethics/analysis; its conditional design questions use four smaller groups in guidance. The thirteen DMP prompts are grouped into materials, protection/documentation, and retention/sharing, with adaptable examples and a continuously generated read-only preview. Focus shows all groups. Moving between modes or steps never clears a field.

Question wording has two distinct states. Typing automatically saves an uncommitted draft. **Save question revision** is the explicit commit action. Finding literature, switching project pages, browser Back and changing mode do not commit a draft. **Discard uncommitted draft** restores the committed wording without creating a version. Committed versions remain reviewable beside the editor and in Project history. A draft may be used to explore literature before finalizing the agreed study question.

The optional research pathway can be quantitative empirical, qualitative empirical, mixed methods or literature-based. It changes examples and prompts, not the saved design, evidence, data source or ethics status. Changing a pathway preserves earlier answers; mismatched pathway/data choices prompt review. Older projects have no inferred pathway. The choice is reversible and does not automatically choose a method or exempt work from institutional review.

Pathway examples are editorial planning aids, not independently validated academic advice. Source-specific reporting and appraisal guidance remain separate. Independent methods/librarian review, physical-device testing and a full manual screen-reader assessment are still separate release gates. No AI service, account, paid API or new runtime dependency is introduced.

Behavior checks cover pathway backup compatibility, explicit drafts/versions, navigation without commits, layout switching with unchanged answers, DMP preview, and browser persistence. Consult `TESTING.md` for completed runs rather than treating test definitions as results.
